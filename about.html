Our Story

Rooted in ancient wisdom, growing through modern care — discover the heart behind Sole Harmony.

Founded in 2014

A Sanctuary Born from Passion

Sole Harmony began as a dream shared by two practitioners — a traditional Chinese medicine practitioner and a Western-trained physiotherapist. United by the belief that the feet hold the key to whole-body wellness, they created a space where ancient healing arts could flourish alongside modern care.

What started as a small studio with two treatment rooms has grown into a beloved wellness destination, serving thousands of clients who seek natural, non-invasive approaches to health and relaxation.

Every element of our studio — from the bamboo textures on our walls to the hand-selected essential oils we use — is chosen with intention. We don't just offer treatments; we curate experiences that honour the body's innate wisdom.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To make the transformative power of reflexology accessible to everyone, providing personalized, evidence-informed treatments in a space that nurtures both body and spirit. We are committed to empowering our clients with knowledge about their own well-being through the language of the feet.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted name in reflexology wellness, recognized globally for our dedication to excellence, innovation in traditional healing, and our role in building a healthier, more balanced world — one step at a time.

Our Journey

Milestones Along the Way

2014

The Beginning

Sole Harmony opens its doors with two treatment rooms and a vision to bring authentic reflexology to the community. Word spreads quickly through personal referrals.

2016

Expansion & Recognition

Awarded "Best Wellness Studio" by the city wellness association. Expanded to six treatment rooms and introduced hot stone therapy and aromatherapy services.

2018

Training Academy Launch

Launched the Sole Harmony Academy, offering certification courses in reflexology to aspiring practitioners. Over 200 students trained in the first two years.

2021

Wellness Retreat Programs

Introduced full-day and weekend retreat programs combining reflexology with meditation, yoga, and nutrition counseling. Partnerships with local organic farms established.

2024

10 Years of Harmony

Celebrated a decade of healing with over 8,000 clients served. Opened a second location and launched an online booking platform. The journey continues.
