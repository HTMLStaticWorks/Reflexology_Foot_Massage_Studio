Explore Reflexology Points

Discover how different areas of the foot correspond to organs and systems in your body. Click a point to learn more.

Point 01

Head & Brain

The tips of the toes correspond to the head and brain. Applying gentle pressure here can help relieve headaches, improve mental clarity, and reduce stress. This is often the starting point of a full reflexology session.

Regular stimulation of these points has been shown to improve sleep quality and reduce symptoms of anxiety.

Point 02

Sinus & Nasal Passages

The base of the toes relates to the sinuses and nasal passages. Targeted pressure in this area helps clear congestion, improve breathing, and relieve sinus headaches naturally.

This technique is particularly beneficial during allergy seasons and cold weather months.

Point 03

Heart & Chest

Located in the ball of the foot, this reflex area is connected to the heart and chest. Gentle stimulation can promote cardiovascular health, improve blood circulation, and ease chest tension.

Our therapists use specialized techniques for this sensitive area to ensure comfort and effectiveness.

Point 04

Stomach & Digestive System

The arch of the foot maps to the digestive system. Reflexology on this area aids digestion, reduces bloating, and can help alleviate symptoms of indigestion and nausea.

Many clients report improved digestive health after just a few sessions focused on this area.

Point 05

Spine & Lower Back

The inner edge of the foot corresponds to the spine. Working these points helps relieve back pain, improve posture, and release tension stored in the spinal column.

This is one of the most requested treatments among office workers and those with sedentary lifestyles.
