Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about reflexology, session expectations, and how we cater to your specific well-being.

How does reflexology differ from regular foot massage?

While traditional foot massage focuses primarily on muscle relaxation, reflexology targets precise reflex points mapped to corresponding organs, glands, and biological systems throughout the whole body.

Is reflexology painful or ticklish?

Reflexology uses firm, grounded thumb-and-finger pressure rather than light strokes, preventing ticklish sensations. If any point feels tender, let your practitioner know and pressure will be customized.

Can reflexology help with plantar fasciitis?

Yes. Our Deep Tissue Reflex and Meridian Balancing sessions are designed to gently release tension in the plantar fascia tendon, calf muscles, and Achilles tendon, significantly alleviating daily discomfort.

How often should I receive treatments?

For chronic stress or pain management, an initial series of weekly sessions produces wonderful cumulative results. For ongoing wellness and balance maintenance, bi-weekly or monthly sessions are ideal.
